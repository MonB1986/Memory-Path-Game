How to play.

Watch the dots light up, then tap the same dots in the right order before the time runs out.

The Rules.

  1. Press the Start button to begin.
  2. Watch the dots light up one at a time.
  3. When they stop, it's your turn.
  4. Tap those dots in the order they lit up.
  5. Get them all right and you'll move to the next round.

How it gets harder.

Round 1 starts with three dots. Each round adds one more, up to a maximum of ten. The same dot can light up more than once, so don't assume every dot will only appear once.

Ways to lose.

  • Tap the wrong dot instead of the one that lit up.
  • Run out of time. You have five seconds for each turn.

Accessibility.

The dots are real button elements, and they are keyboard reachable. Each dot is at least 44 pixels, making them easier to tap on mobile devices.
